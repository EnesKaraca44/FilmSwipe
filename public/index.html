Privacy Policy for FilmSwipe

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our FilmSwipe mobile application (the "Service") and the choices you have associated with that data.

Information Collection and Use

We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number. You may choose a nickname, which is shown to the people you are matched with. Our application uses the following services to function and improve your experience:

  • Firebase Authentication (Anonymous): To provide a unique and persistent identity for each user without collecting personal information. This allows the app to function in a multiplayer environment. Your anonymous user ID is stored securely by Google.
  • Firebase Realtime Database: To store real-time game data, such as movie selections and chat messages, associated with your anonymous user ID.
  • Firebase Cloud Functions: To process game logic, such as determining a match, on the server-side.
  • Google AdMob: To show banner ads to users who have not purchased the ad-free version. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form and can change their choice at any time from Settings > "Ad privacy options". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Google Play Billing: To process the optional "Remove Ads" purchase. Payment details are handled by Google; we never see them.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB): Movie information and posters are loaded from the TMDB API. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: To collect anonymous usage data, such as which features are used most often, to help us improve the app. This data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify you personally.

Chat, Reports and Blocking

Matched users can chat with each other. Before chatting you must accept our community rules: harassment, hate speech, threats, sexual content, spam and sharing personal information are not allowed. You can report a message or a user and block them from inside the chat. Reports (the reported message, the anonymous IDs involved and the reason) are stored so that we can review them and remove users who break the rules. Blocked users are stored only on your device.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • Game rooms, movie choices and chat messages are deleted automatically within about 24 hours after the room is created.
  • Matchmaking queue entries are removed when you leave the queue or disconnect.
  • Reports are kept only as long as needed to review them.
  • Your watchlist, nickname and settings are stored only on your device. Signing out from Settings removes your nickname and starts a new anonymous identity; uninstalling the app removes all local data.
  • To request deletion of any data associated with your anonymous ID, contact us at the address below.

Children

FilmSwipe is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
